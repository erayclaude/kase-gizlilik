Privacy Policy

kaşe keeps your payment information only on your phone. No account, no server of ours; we never see the payments you add.

01What this policy covers

This text explains how the kaşe mobile app (the "app") handles personal data. It also serves as the information notice under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Data controller: Eray Kaçmaz, Bursa, Türkiye. Contact: kasedestek@gmail.com

02Your information stays on your phone

The payments you add (name, amount, category, due date, frequency, paid status and amount history) and your settings (reminder time, language, currency, plan status) are stored only on your own device.

This information is not sent to us or to any other server. The app has no account creation, sign-in or cloud sync.

03Notifications

Due date reminders are "local notifications": your phone sets the alarm itself, without using the internet or a server.

We ask for permission before sending notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

04Ads (free plan)

On the free plan, ads are shown through Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, AdMob may process information such as your device's advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android), IP address, device model, operating system and interactions with ads. The payments you add are not shared with AdMob.

On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Where required, such as in the EEA and UK, we ask for your consent for ads, and you can change it in the app's settings.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

No ads are shown on the Pro plan.

05Purchases

Pro purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; Apple or Google handles them.

To verify whether Pro is active, our subscription infrastructure provider RevenueCat processes a randomly generated anonymous ID and purchase records. Your name and email address are not part of this process. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

06Exporting

When you use "Export to Excel", your payments are sent by you to the app you choose (for example email or messaging). This sharing does not pass through us.

07Deleting your data

You can delete everything you added under Settings > Delete all payments. Removing the app from your phone also deletes all your information. Since we have no copy, it cannot be recovered.

08Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, request information, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object. If you live in the EEA or UK, you have similar rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion and objection, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. Since your payment information is not held by us, you can exercise these rights directly in the app. For requests about ad and purchase data, you can write to us; we will also help you reach the relevant provider.

09Children

kaşe is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

10Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page and the date at the top is updated. For important changes, we will also let you know in the app.

11Contact

For questions: kasedestek@gmail.com
